## About This Post

Lately on Xiaohongshu (Rednote), I stumbled upon a post sharing chatGPT prompts that create posters for uploaded images. The expected final output will be a high-end，3:4 vertical poster, with the top half featuring a faithfully preserved version of the uploaded images (enhanced only with color grading), and the bottom half being a minimalist, hand-drawn illustration on warm, off-white paper. The overall visual effect creates a poetic, postercard-like and artistic book-cover aesthetic. The link of the original post is attached at the end of this blog.

I applied these prompts to generate several posters for pictures taken during my 2-week trip to Japan last summer. Here, I post them up and let's enjoy together.
